# SpeakStep 3D: Monster Realm Expedition **Comprehensive Game Documentation & Pedagogical Overview** --- ## 1. Executive Summary & Purpose **SpeakStep 3D: Monster Realm Expedition** is an immersive, gamified English Language Speaking Application built using Three.js and Web Speech API technologies. ### Core Purpose: * **Overcoming Foreign Language Anxiety (FLA):** Traditional oral speaking exercises often induce anxiety in young ESL/EFL learners. By converting speech practice into "Voice Spells" required to defeat 3D monsters, learners shift their focus from social judgment to game progression. * **Scaffolding Oral Fluency:** The game addresses varying proficiency levels by offering three distinct scaffold tiers (Level 1: Explorer, Level 2: Adventurer, Level 3: Champion) featuring adaptive sentence frames, dynamic word banks, and real-time audio models. * **Interactive Active Learning:** Instead of passive drill-and-practice, learners physically navigate a 3D island terrain using platforming elements (jumping over obstacles, running between themed stations) to trigger active speaking challenges. --- ## 2. Core Game Mechanics & Exploration Architecture ### A. 3D World Navigation * **3rd-Person Camera System:** Smooth, dynamic tracking camera attached to a custom 3D articulated explorer avatar. * **Platforming & Movement:** Fully interactive keyboard controls (`WASD` / Arrow keys for movement, `SPACE` for jump physics, `E` for interaction) alongside touch-enabled virtual joysticks and jump/interact buttons for mobile devices. * **Proximity Triggers:** Approaching any of the 6 Monster Stations detects player distance and renders interactive UI prompts without interrupting 3D rendering. ### B. The 6 Monster Stations & Syllabic Progression The game environment features 6 distinct stations, each guarded by a thematic boss monster designed to target specific English communication standards: | Station ID | Title | Boss Monster | Target Speaking Skill / Learning Objective | | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | | **Station 1** | Garden of Lost Words | *Gloom Golem* | Descriptive language, adjectives, basic observation (`I can see...`). | | **Station 2** | Bridge of Broken Sentences | *Vortex Serpent* | Reasoned opinions & connective conjunctions (`...because it is...`). | | **Station 3** | Echo Caves | *Cursed Echo Behemoth* | Vocabulary synthesis & pronunciation clarity. | | **Station 4** | Island of Curious Questions | *Shadow Kraken Guard* | Extended responses & situational details (Animals, habitats, routines). | | **Station 5** | Storykeeper Memory Maze | *Nightmare Story Weaver* | Sequential storytelling using discourse markers (`First`, `Next`, `Finally`). | | **Station 6** | The Heart of Verba | *Void Sovereign* | Comprehensive open presentation & reflective speaking. | --- ## 3. Educational Mechanics & Scaffolding System ### A. Tiered Scaffolding (Difficulty Alignment) learners choose their entry tier at launch, which alters the UI during monster battles: 1. **Level 1 (Explorer):** Provides strict sentence frames with blank slots and simple word bubbles to lower cognitive load. 2. **Level 2 (Adventurer):** Offers intermediate templates encouraging clause linking and compound sentences. 3. **Level 3 (Champion):** Provides open storytelling prompts requiring independent construction of detailed responses. ### B. Speech Recognition & Real-Time Feedback Loop 1. **Target Word Detection:** Uses native browser speech recognition (`Web Speech API`) to record learner responses. 2. **Keyword & Structure Analysis:** Checks spoken input against target vocabulary banks. Using connective words like *"because"* unlocks "Critical Strike" and bonus damage visual effects. 3. **Fallback System:** On devices lacking speech recognition support or mic permissions, an interactive manual spell text input ensures uninterrupted gameplay. 4. **Text-To-Speech (TTS) Exemplars:** Includes an integrated audio guide allowing learners to listen to target vocabulary models before casting their "Voice Spell". --- ## 4. Assessment & Rewards Mechanics * **Verba Energy (VE) & Progress HUD:** Defeating each monster awards **100 VE** and liberates the respective station barrier mesh in real-time. * **Station Boss Map:** Offers immediate teleportation to unlocked stations, keeping track of completed vs. remaining challenges. * **Expedition Certificate:** Upon defeating all 6 boss monsters, the game compiles player stats into an official printable certificate detailing level achieved, total VE earned, and monster stations liberated.
